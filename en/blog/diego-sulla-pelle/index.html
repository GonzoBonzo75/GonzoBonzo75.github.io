Diego on his skin

12 November 2026

On Thursdays, a piece of my work. I begin with a photograph from the third Scudetto: “Diego on his skin”.

It is 4 May 2023, 18:41, in Naples. The afternoon is still giving way to evening and the city has already stopped pretending. A blue rises from the alleys that is not a colour, it is a temperature. People walk without a destination, and every corner holds a chorus.

In the middle of all this, a bare side. And on the skin, a face: the curls, the gaze of Diego Armando Maradona. It is not painted on a wall or sewn onto a flag. It is under the skin, where it does not come off.

With him Napoli had won two Scudetti, in 1987 and 1990. Then thirty-three years of waiting, and in between a death, in November 2020, that the city mourned the way one mourns in a family. The stadium had taken his name. That evening his name was not needed: he was everywhere, on the walls, on the flags, in the voices. And on a man’s side.

All around, the crowd dissolves into a smear of colours: blue, pink, green, shadows of people passing. The tattoo, instead, stays sharp. It is the only still thing in a city in motion: a face that does not age, carried by a man who will.

A city had been waiting for thirty-three years, and for one night it stopped counting. In this photograph the past and the present share the same frame: one on the skin, the other out of focus.
