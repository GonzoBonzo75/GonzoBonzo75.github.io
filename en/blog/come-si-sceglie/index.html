How to choose one photograph out of 92

27 November 2026

One walk, ninety-two photographs. You need a dozen. How do you do it?

First pass, quick: I remove the repeats. If I shot the same scene three times, one stays. Usually it is not the one I thought.

Second pass: I remove the beautiful photographs that say the same thing as another. A beautiful photograph that repeats does not serve the story.

Third pass: I look at what is missing. A story needs a place, a sign of people’s presence, a detail and a breath. Often the most useful photograph is the most modest.

Then the photo editor’s rule: every photograph must have a reason. If I cannot say it in one sentence, it goes.

Finally the order. A sequence is not a list: each photograph must answer the one before.

And then I let it rest. The next day, one or two change places.
